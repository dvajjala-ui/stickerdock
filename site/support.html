SDStickerDock support

Help with your stickers

Contact the developer at dvjjala@gmail.com for support, private data-access/deletion requests, or Pinterest connection questions. Please describe the feature and approximate date; do not send passwords, API keys or owner access tokens.

Your Android library

Imported stickers stay on your device. Export a backup before uninstalling or changing between debug and release installations. The developer cannot recover an unexported local library from the server.

Cloud files and Pinterest

For private Studio files, contact us with the source filename or request ID. We can arrange deletion of the private source/result; public pack removals affect future downloads, not copies already saved by recipients. Disconnect Pinterest in Admin to remove the stored OAuth credential, and revoke access in Pinterest settings. Pin/Board results are fetched live and are not archived.
