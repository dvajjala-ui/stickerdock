What works today

StickerDock supports personal image/folder/ZIP import, static artwork editing, captions, search, categories, backup and pack sharing. Creator Studio and the owner admin can upload sources, queue AI-assisted captions, review drafts and publish general-audience packs to the Android catalog.

Groq analyzes selected source images and suggests short captions. Local rendering produces WebP stickers. Original AI image generation and native AI caption suggestions remain in development.

Pinterest connection

The optional integration requests read-only access to the authenticated owner's boards and Pins through official OAuth. Admin reads these live and links back to Pinterest. Pinterest access requires platform approval and a valid connection. StickerDock does not archive Pin data or images, train models using Pinterest content, or turn Pins into distributable stickers. Meme generation uses independently uploaded or licensed sources instead.

Personal library privacy

Phone imports remain available offline. Sign in and enable optional Private cloud sync to back up general-audience packs with categories, labels and tags, then restore them on another installation. Unknown categories use All. Mature packs stay offline. Public sharing is a separate opt-in requiring rights confirmation, moderation and deliberate publication.
